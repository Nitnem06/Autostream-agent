## Core Features

### AI Video Editing
AutoStream uses machine learning to automatically cut silences, remove filler words (um, uh, like), and identify highlight moments in long-form content. Save up to 80% editing time.

### Auto-Captions
Generate accurate captions in 50+ languages with speaker detection. Customize fonts, colors, and positioning. Export as SRT, VTT, or burned-in.

### Platform Integrations
Direct publishing to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Twitch. Auto-resize for each platform's aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16, 1:1).

### Smart Clips
AI detects the most engaging 30-60 second moments from long videos and creates short-form clips automatically — perfect for TikTok and Reels.

### Voice Enhancement
Remove background noise, normalize audio levels, and enhance voice clarity with one click.
